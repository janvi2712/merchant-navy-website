⚓ Check Eligibility – Merchant Navy

🔹 Age Criteria

  • Minimum Age: 17.5 years
  • Maximum Age: 25 years
  • Age relaxation may apply for some courses

🔹 Educational Qualification

  • 10th Pass – for Rating courses
  • 12th Pass (PCM) – for Officer level courses
  • Minimum 50–60% marks (varies by course)

🔹 Medical Requirements

  • Physically & mentally fit
  • No color blindness
  • Good eyesight (as per DG Shipping norms)

🔹 Nationality

  • Indian Citizen
  • Valid passport mandatory

🔹 Documents Required

  • 10th / 12th Marksheet
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport (or appointment slip)
  • Medical fitness certificate
