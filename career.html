Career Timeline (Deck Department)

Deck Cadet

Entry-level trainee responsible for learning navigation, watchkeeping, safety, and ship operations. After sea-time and exams, Deck Cadets become Junior Officers.

Deck Officer

Responsible for navigation, communication, chart planning, cargo operations, and bridge watch duties.

Officer / Engineer

Officers are responsible for navigation and watch duties. Engineers handle ship machinery and engine systems.

Chief Officer / Second Engineer

Senior management roles responsible for deck operations or engine room management.

Captain / Chief Engineer

Highest rank at sea. Captain controls the vessel and Chief Engineer manages ship propulsion & power systems.

Salary Progression

Cadet: ₹40k – ₹80k/month
Officer: ₹1–3 Lakhs/month
Chief Officer: ₹4–6 Lakhs/month
Captain: ₹7–12 Lakhs/month
