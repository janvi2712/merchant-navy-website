What is Merchant Navy?

The Merchant Navy is the commercial shipping industry that transports cargo, goods, oil, fuel, and passengers across the world using large ships. More than 90% of international trade takes place through merchant ships, making it one of the most essential global industries.

Unlike the Indian Navy (which focuses on national defense and maritime security), the Merchant Navy is a civilian sector focused on transportation, logistics, and global shipping operations.

How the Merchant Navy Works

Merchant ships operate between countries and continents, transporting essential commodities such as oil, automobiles, food supplies, and heavy machinery. Skilled officers and crew members ensure the ship's navigation, safety, engineering, and operations.

Departments in Merchant Navy

Deck Department

Handles navigation, cargo, and vessel safety. Career path: Cadet → Officer → Chief Officer → Captain

Engine Department

Manages ship machinery, power & engine systems. Career path: Trainee → Officer → 2nd Engineer → Chief Engineer

Catering Department

Provides hospitality, food, and crew services onboard. Career path: Saloon Rating → Steward → Chief Cook

Career Growth & Salary

Merchant Navy careers offer structured promotions and high salaries. Growth is based on experience, training, and performance at sea.

Cadet: ₹40k – ₹80k/month
Officer: ₹1 – ₹3 Lakhs/month
Chief Officer: ₹4 – ₹6 Lakhs/month
Captain/Chief Engineer: ₹7 – ₹12 Lakhs+/month

Conclusion
